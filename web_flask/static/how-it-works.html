HOW IT WORKS

The Engineering Behind AutoBus AI

From raw camera frames to auditable autonomous driving decisions — explore the complete perception, intelligence, and decision pipeline that powers safer urban transit.

CORE TECHNICAL PIPELINE

Six Engineered Modules

Each module is a self-contained stage in the AutoBus AI perception-to-action pipeline, running synchronously under 50 ms.

Module 01

360° Multi-Camera Perception

YOLOv8-based object detection across five synchronized camera viewpoints: Front, Front-Left, Front-Right, Rear-Left, and Rear-Right. Combines all five viewpoints to provide continuous 360° environmental perception around the bus.

YOLOv8 Detection 5 Calibrated Perspectives Sub-50ms Latency
Module 02

Traffic Signal Recognition

HSV-based color classification from detected traffic lights. Detected traffic-light regions are analyzed using HSV color information to classify red, yellow, and green signal states.

HSV Color Segmentation Deterministic Safety Interlock
Module 03

Blind Spot Intelligence

Lane occupancy and collision-risk scoring for safer lane changes. Evaluates proximity, relative velocity, and Time-To-Collision (TTC) to determine safe clearance buffers.

Time-to-Collision (TTC) Lane Occupancy Flag 0-100 Risk Scoring
Module 04

Adaptive Sensor Fusion

Brightness, contrast and blur-based camera reliability estimation dynamically weights camera inputs into a fusion confidence score. Unreliable feeds receive lower trust while reliable feeds contribute more strongly.

Blur / Contrast / Brightness Dynamic Sensor Trust Self-Healing Reliability
Module 05

RL Decision Engine

A policy-driven decision engine that selects actions based on the current scene and safety state. Supported actions: MOVE, SLOW_DOWN, STOP, CHANGE_LEFT, and CHANGE_RIGHT.

Scenario-Based Policy 5 Discrete Output States Safety Reward Metrics
Module 06

Decision Fusion Layer

Arbitrates between reinforcement-learning output and risk-based safety rules to produce a single final decision, speed recommendation and lane action, together with a human-readable explanation.

Deterministic Safety Interlock Speed & Lane Recommendations Human-Readable Auditing

Continuously evaluates lane occupancy, approaching vehicle velocity, and collision risk during planned turns or lane changes. Dynamically calculates Time-To-Collision (TTC) to inhibit dangerous side impacts.

Capability 04 • Self-Healing Vision

Adaptive Sensor Fusion

Measures real-time lens degradation across all cameras using blur variance, contrast spread, and brightness distribution. Obscured or rain-splattered lenses are down-weighted dynamically while pristine camera streams receive boosted trust.
