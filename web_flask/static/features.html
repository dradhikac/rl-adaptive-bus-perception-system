AUTONOMOUS CAPABILITIES

What the AutoBus AI System Provides

Deployable capabilities engineered to protect passengers, detect blind-spot hazards, respect traffic signals, and make auditable driving decisions.

Capability 01 • Surround Vision

360° Multi-Camera Perception

Provides real-time spatial awareness across five synchronized hardware camera streams. Combines front-facing long-range optics with peripheral and rear views to ensure zero blind spots around the entire bus chassis.

  • Simultaneous multi-stream inference identifying buses, auto-rickshaws, pedestrians, and cyclists
  • Eliminates high-risk blind zones during turns and curbside bus-stop maneuvers
Capability 02 • Signal Compliance

Traffic Signal Recognition

Recognizes and classifies traffic-light states in real time using visual color classification in HSV space. Enforces strict zero-violation intersection interlocks to guarantee compliance with municipal signals.

Capability 03 • Spatial Safety

Blind Spot Intelligence

Continuously evaluates lane occupancy, approaching vehicle velocity, and collision risk during planned turns or lane changes. Dynamically calculates Time-To-Collision (TTC) to inhibit dangerous side impacts.

Capability 04 • Self-Healing Vision

Adaptive Sensor Fusion

Measures real-time lens degradation across all cameras using blur variance, contrast spread, and brightness distribution. Obscured or rain-splattered lenses are down-weighted dynamically while pristine camera streams receive boosted trust.

Laplacian Quality Scoring

  • Continuously recalibrates weights as the bus exits adverse weather or tunnels

Reinforcement Learning Decision Engine

Trained on complex real-world urban transit scenarios to output tactical decisions based on the current scene and safety state. Discrete action space provides stable, passenger-comfortable transitions between five supported states: MOVE, SLOW_DOWN, STOP, CHANGE_LEFT, and CHANGE_RIGHT.

Decision Fusion
