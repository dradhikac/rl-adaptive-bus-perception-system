Autonomous Bus Perception Architecture

The system implements a synchronized multi-modal perception pipeline engineered specifically for unpredictable urban bus corridors, fusing vision, tracking, and reinforcement learning.

Phase 1 & 2 • Detection

Fine-Tuned YOLOv8

Trained across FGVD and IDD-Lite Indian driving datasets with specialized bus, auto-rickshaw, and pedestrian zoning.

Phase 3 • Tracking

ByteTrack Multi-Object Tracking

Real-time low-score association maintaining persistent track IDs across 30 FPS camera feeds.

Phase 4 • Temporal

Motion Intent GRU

Classifies trajectory history into approaching-road, crossing, stationary, receding, and erratic behaviors.

Phase 5 • Stops & Docking

Bus Stop Geofencing

Fuses visual stop signs, commuter crowd clustering, and GPS proximity with a multi-state docking state machine.

Phase 6 • Safety Zones

Door Zone Safety Monitor

Interior/entry camera monitoring passenger boarding/alighting with interlocked departure prevention.

Phase 7 • Arbitration

Decision Fusion & RL Policy

Dynamic arbitration between rule-based safety interlocks and reinforcement learning action policies.
